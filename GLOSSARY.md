# Cadence

An online course platform where learners buy courses, watch lessons, and can ask for their money back.

## Language

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask, in their own words, to get their money back for a Purchase. It is Requested until staff Approve or Decline it.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, cancel request, Reversal, Chargeback, Pending (for the Requested state)

**Refund**:
The money returned to a learner after a Refund Request is Approved. It is always the amount the learner actually paid, and it ends the Purchase along with access to the course.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Cancellation

**Refund Window**:
The 14 calendar days after a Purchase during which a Refund Request can be raised. The last day counts in full.
_Avoid_: Cancel window

**Refund Eligibility**:
Whether a Purchase can still get a Refund Request: it is inside the Refund Window, the learner has watched under 2 hours and completed under half the lessons, the seat is not a claimed team seat, and no Refund Request is already open.
_Avoid_: Cancellation offer

**Chargeback**:
A dispute the cardholder raises with their bank, which takes the money back without our say. It is not a Refund Request and is not modelled yet.
